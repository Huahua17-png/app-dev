## Overview
**Hunter x Hunter** is a critically acclaimed anime and manga series following a young boy named **Gon Freecss** as he strives to become a licensed *Hunter* to find his missing father.

### Main Characters
* **Gon Freecss**[cite: 1] - An energetic and determined young boy with exceptional intuition.
* **Killua Zoldyck**[cite: 1] - Gon's best friend and a former assassin from a legendary family of killers.
* **Kurapika**[cite: 1] - The last surviving member of the Kurta Clan, seeking vengeance.
* **Leorio Paradinight**[cite: 1] - An aspiring doctor who joins the Hunter Exam for medical school tuition.

---
### Key Arc Highlights
1. **The Hunter Exam Arc** - Meeting friends and facing brutal trials.
2. **Heaven's Arena Arc** - Learning *Nen* abilities.
3. **Chimera Ant Arc** - A profound psychological and tactical conflict.
